\# Skill: review-architecture



Evaluate whether the current change is architecturally appropriate.



\## Review checklist

1\. Responsibility boundaries

2\. Dependency direction

3\. Ownership of logic

4\. Coupling introduced

5\. Scalability of the approach

6\. Appropriateness of trade-offs for current scope



\## Rules

\- Be pragmatic.

\- Do not demand unnecessary redesign.

\- Clearly separate acceptable debt from unacceptable risk.



\## Deliverable

Return:

\- strengths

\- risks

\- accepted trade-offs

\- unacceptable trade-offs

\- recommendation
